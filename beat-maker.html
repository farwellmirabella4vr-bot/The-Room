Beat Maker

An 8-pad sampler for your own sounds — tap, record, save, export.

Sampler

Each pad is its own category — drag or upload several files onto one pad (e.g. all your kicks) and tap it repeatedly to cycle through them, or use the small ▸ arrow. New samples open the trim tool first; use the ✂ icon on a pad to re-trim what's currently selected. Download packs from Splice (or anywhere) and drag them in here — direct Splice account connection isn't available, but any sample file works.

Visualizer

Reacts to everything passing through the master output — pads, the sequencer, saved-beat playback, and radio capture.

FX Rack

Click the ⚙ gear icon on a pad to edit its filter, delay, and reverb sends. Changes apply live and are baked into exports.
