✦ The Road to Spain III · The roadmap

Seven phases, in order

The next unchecked box is the only one that matters today. Check it, and the next lights up.

01

Foundation

Sept 2026 → spring 2027
  • Open a dedicated Spain savings account; hold the monthly target
  • Passport valid for the first year in Spain + 3 months
  • Choose the city with the degree in mind — Alicante, Málaga or Granada
  • Enrol at a university language centre, never a private academy
  • Order the FBI check (12+ weeks), then apostille + translate it
02

The student visa

Consulate of Spain, Los Angeles · via BLS
  • Spain-compliant health insurance — no co-pay, no wait, no cap
  • Medical certificate in the consulate’s wording
  • Proof of funds: 100% IPREM (€600/mo in 2026) × months of stay
  • Book BLS the day the file is complete — submit ≥ 2 months before class
03

Land

within weeks of approval
  • Fly once the visa is stamped; first month of housing lined up
  • Empadronamiento at the town hall; TIE card within 30 days
  • Bank account, local SIM, and find the mosque
04

Build Spanish

through 2027
  • Near-perfect attendance — the visa depends on it
  • A2, then B1; sit a mock DELE, then book DELE or SIELE
  • Arabic every day, from day one
05

Into the university

2027 → autumn 2028
  • Confirm International Relations entry rules at 2–3 universities
  • Apostille + translate the high-school transcript & diploma
  • UNEDasiss recognition + any PCE subject exams
  • Apply in the spring window; renew the stay as a degree student
06

The degree years

2028 → 2032
  • Arabic to a testable level (ACTFL / ILR); a Casa Árabe course
  • A summer internship every year; choose a focus
07

The service

2032 onward
  • The Foreign Service exam; think tanks; roles that pay for Spanish + Arabic
  • Decide — build from Spain, or return — with real offers in hand
